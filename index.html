Part-time minimums are scaled automatically. Provisional E/D maximum is year 8.

CPP applies at 75%–100% appointment with no cap noted in conference guidance. UMLife Options is a CPP alternative for 50%-time appointments only. Compass applies at 50%-time and above; UMPIP for below 50%-time. The parsonage minimum value ($10,000) sets a floor on the 35% parsonage bump when computing total compensation — if 35% of salary is less than this amount, the floor is used instead (verbally confirmed NE practice; not yet documented in NEAC source materials). Set to $0 to disable. The HealthFlex ACH rate is a discount for churches billed by ACH (bank draft). Verify all rates against current NEAC Benefits Office guidance annually.
